# Product Context: @ooraculodabola

## Purpose
An automated high-performance football news platform for Instagram that delivers summaries with a "mystical authority" and "data-driven" aesthetic.

## Users
Hardcore football fans, bettors, and sports enthusiasts who value speed, accuracy, and a premium visual experience that stands out in a crowded feed.

## Brand Identity
- **Tone:** Authoritative, Oracle-like, mysterious but technically precise.
- **Voice:** Direct, confident, using "football-slang" mixed with analytical terms.
- **Register:** Brand (Design is the product).

## Strategic Principles
1. **The Oracle never fails:** The summaries must be sharp and the layout must feel "predicted" and perfect.
2. **High Impact:** Use high contrast and vibrant accents.
3. **Data Over Slop:** No generic designs. Use structural grids and technical textures.

## Register Reference
- **Strategy:** Committed (One saturated color carries 30–60% of the surface).
- **Theme:** Dark Mode (Scene: Fan in a dim environment, smartphone brightness high, needs maximum legibility and "cool" factor).
