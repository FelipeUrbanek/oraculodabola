# Product Context: Oráculo Digital Ecosystem

An autonomous, high-fidelity media engine running parallel pipelines for specialized Instagram news.

---

## ⚽ Brand 1: @ooraculodabola (Futebol)

### Purpose
An automated high-performance football news platform that delivers breaking news and tactical summaries with a "mystical authority" and analytical data-driven aesthetic.

### Users
Hardcore football fans, bettors, and sports enthusiasts who value speed, accuracy, and a premium visual experience that stands out in a crowded sports feed.

### Brand Identity & Voice
- **Tone:** Authoritative, Oracle-like, mysterious but technically precise.
- **Voice:** Direct, confident, analytical, using sharp Portuguese sports-slang mixed with statistical terms.
- **Register:** Brand (Design IS the product).
- **Color Strategy:** Committed (Tinted neutrals + vibrant Electric Pitch Green).
- **Physical Scene:** Fan in a dim environment, smartphone brightness high, needs maximum legibility and "cool" factor.

---

## 🎬 Brand 2: @espectadorcomum (Cinema & Séries)

### Purpose
An avant-garde, premium pop culture, movie, and series delivery pipeline. It offers highly curated review cards, breaking cast news, and behind-the-scenes glimpses with an elegant editorial design.

### Users
Moviegoers, series binge-watchers, and pop-culture enthusiasts looking for quick, aesthetic, and trustworthy recommendations and news about what to watch today.

### Brand Identity & Voice
- **Tone:** Sophisticated, conversational, artsy, graphic-designer-crafted.
- **Voice:** Enthusiastic, knowledgeable, engaging, resembling a cool movie critic friend.
- **Register:** Brand (Design IS the product).
- **Color Strategy:** Full Palette & Drenched (Multiple bespoke OKLCH accent highlights per category - orange, purple, cyan, red - with 10 completely diverse, bespoke templates).
- **Physical Scene:** Cinephile browsing movie cards in a dimly lit room, looking for high-prestige cinematic contrast.

---

## 🛡️ Strategic Execution Principles

1. **Absolute Channel Isolation:** Never mix content streams. Football always publishes to `@ooraculodabola` via `main.ts` and `IG_USER_ID`. Cinema always publishes to `@espectadorcomum` via `main_cinema.ts` and `IG_USER_ID_CINEMA`.
2. **Visual Contrast over Monotony:** The 10 cinema layout presets must employ distinct typography (Serif, Sans, Slab, Sans-condensed) to look like bespoke editorial covers.
3. **No Brand Slop:** Strictly reject any generic Open Graph placeholders, favicons, or company branding logos from source web scrapers.
