# Design System: @ooraculodabola

## Colors (OKLCH)
- **Neutral (Primary):** `oklch(15% 0.01 240)` - Deep Charcoal/Night Blue.
- **Accent (Vibrant):** `oklch(75% 0.25 145)` - Electric Pitch Green (Dynamic, can be changed by Gemini).
- **Secondary:** `oklch(25% 0.02 240)` - Glass background.

## Typography
- **Headlines:** `Outfit`, sans-serif, Black (900) weight. Tracking: -0.05em.
- **Body:** `Inter`, sans-serif, Medium (500) weight.
- **Metadata:** Monospace (JetBrains Mono) for a "technical/data" feel.

## Components: News Card
- **Radius:** 2.5rem (Ultra-rounded for a modern mobile app feel).
- **Glassmorphism:** `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` with 10% opacity white borders.
- **Glows:** Strategic radial gradients behind the text to simulate "Oracle" energy.
